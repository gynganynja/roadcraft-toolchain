# D11: preserved project evidence and specialist diagnostics

D11 is one mod project in the general RoadCraft toolchain. Its source repairs,
evidence and transaction history remain at their existing paths. They are not
defaults for creating a different mod or modifying another author's package.

## Continue D11 explicitly

The historical registry is still `project.json` at the toolchain root, with its
original accepted/candidate pins and relative source evidence paths unchanged.
Pass its full path with `--registry` or `ROADCRAFT_REGISTRY`; supply the correct
local machine config explicitly. The current checkout may not have that config.
Do not infer a machine path or accepted hash from an old report.

The earlier repository `C:\RC\D11_Development` contains history and uncommitted
work. Preserve it. Ignored source/assets are not disposable. Current transaction
receipts, locks and live identities take precedence over dated directory names.

## Findings that remain applicable

- Source lineage must be explicit. D11's three repaired-USF variants keep their
  repaired `export.usf` as build inputs; historical `.blend` files are references
  until the asset-specific native-Blender parity gate passes. Preserve geometry,
  material, animation, compiled and native-view evidence for that gate.
- Preserve duplicate ZIP member occurrences and pinned byte identity. Source
  inventories and manifests are evidence, not values to repin for convenience.
- A successful rollback does not validate the failed candidate. D11 candidate
  `aaa-20260928` retains its failed capture and remains unaccepted even after the
  accepted installed package was restored. Keep captured, numerical, visual and
  campaign conclusions separate.
- Native probes require matching scene, camera, lighting, resolution, protocol
  and executable evidence. A test name or phase label does not prove a native
  capture occurred. Temporary scratch diagnostics do not approve native export.
- Source migration needs verified pins and sufficient destination/reserve space.
  Exact-file storage reports identify review candidates, never implicit deletion.

These rules apply when the same source/evidence conditions arise in other mods.
Exact D11 asset names, authoring pins, variant mappings, scene IDs, output paths
and previous failure states stay specific to D11.
